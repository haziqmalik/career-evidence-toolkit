# Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Career Evidence Toolkit is an open-source, skills-only plugin maintained by Hazique Malik. It provides instructions, templates, and local scripts for organizing career evidence and generating resumes. The project does not operate a hosted service, database, analytics system, or remote MCP server.

## Information the toolkit may process

When a user chooses to provide it, the toolkit may work with career-related information such as:

- name and contact details;
- employment, education, skills, projects, and accomplishments;
- professional certificates and public credential records;
- resume templates, profile images, job descriptions, and application documents;
- public professional profile information that the user has authorized the agent to access.

The toolkit does not require passwords, API keys, access tokens, payment information, or government identification. Users should not place secrets in prompts, configuration files, uploaded documents, issues, or the public repository.

## Where information is stored

The toolkit is designed to keep personal career information outside the public plugin repository. Users choose the storage location, such as a local folder or a connected storage provider available to their agent.

The open-source toolkit itself does not receive, host, retain, sell, or share user career information. ChatGPT, Codex, connected storage providers, browsers, and other services used with the toolkit may process information under their own privacy policies and account settings.

## External access

The workflows require user permission before accessing public professional profiles, connected storage, or other external sources. Permission to read a source does not authorize editing, uploading, publishing, or sharing information.

Users are responsible for reviewing the permissions and privacy practices of every connected service they enable.

## Generated documents

Generated resumes and career records may contain personal information. Users control where these files are stored and shared and should inspect them before sending them to employers or publishing them.

## Logs and telemetry

Career Evidence Toolkit does not include independent analytics or telemetry. The agent platform, operating system, or connected services may maintain operational logs according to their own policies.

## Children

The toolkit is intended for professional career-management use and is not directed to children.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published in this repository and identified through version history or release notes.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at:

https://github.com/haziqmalik/career-evidence-toolkit/issues

Do not include resumes, certificates, contact details, credentials, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
