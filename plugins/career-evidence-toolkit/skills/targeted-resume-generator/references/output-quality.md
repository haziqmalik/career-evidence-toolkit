# Output quality

Before final delivery verify:

- every factual claim traces to approved evidence;
- claim strength and project maturity are preserved;
- selected work, projects, and credentials are relevant and non-duplicative;
- chronology and historical titles are accurate;
- the document contains no unresolved placeholders;
- the result is one or two pages only;
- no clipping, overlap, orphan heading, stranded bullet, or nearly empty spill page exists;
- text remains selectable and logically ordered;
- ATS mode remains conventional and single-column;
- Executive mode is selective rather than a decorative copy of ATS content.

Do not repair layout with invented content, unreadably small text, or crushed spacing.
