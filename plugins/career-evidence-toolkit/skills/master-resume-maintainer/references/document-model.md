# Master resume document model

Prefer these semantic sections when supported:

1. Identity and professional positioning
2. Core value proposition
3. Professional experience
4. Selected projects and engineering or domain work
5. Cross-project capabilities
6. Technical or professional skills
7. Leadership and delivery
8. Certifications
9. Education
10. Verification notes

Update existing sections before creating duplicates. Preserve useful historical work. Notes may retain uncertainty but public-facing claims must not present unverified information as fact.
