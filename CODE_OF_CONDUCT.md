# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Career Evidence Toolkit is an open-source project intended to be useful to people from different professional, technical, cultural, and educational backgrounds.

Project participation should remain respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the software and documentation.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- giving technical feedback clearly and respectfully;
- disagreeing with ideas without attacking people;
- keeping examples and test fixtures synthetic rather than exposing personal data;
- respecting privacy and permission boundaries;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them; and
- helping new contributors understand project conventions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, deliberate disclosure of private information, spam, or sustained disruptive behavior.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other public collaboration channels associated with Career Evidence Toolkit.

## Enforcement

Potential conduct or privacy concerns should be reported privately to the project maintainer rather than escalated through public argument. Reports will be reviewed in context and proportionate action may include a warning, removal of content, closing or locking a thread, or restricting participation.

Security vulnerabilities should be handled according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through public issues.
